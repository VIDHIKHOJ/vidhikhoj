Shreya Singhal v. Union of India

Date: 24 March 2015

Citation: AIR 2015 SC 1523

This landmark Supreme Court judgment dealt with Section 66A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its constitutional validity in relation to freedom of speech and expression under Article 19(1)(a) of the Constitution of India.
